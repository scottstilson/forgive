# forgive *verb*

**1 a :** to dismiss the sins or debts of (a person) as grounds for harboring ill will toward or continuing to claim punishment of, repayment from, or [amends](https://github.com/scottstilson/amends) from 
> “Father, _forgive_ them, for they don’t know what they’re doing” (Luke 23:34)

**&nbsp;&nbsp;b :** to forgive (sense 1a) the person responsible for (a sin or debt)
> “Look at my misery and my trouble / And _forgive_ all my sins” (Psalm 25:18).
